5 Literature & interpretation

Searches PubMed for the strongest genes, then asks a language model to interpret the results using only those abstracts and the pathways above.

Only gene symbols, fold changes and p-values are sent to PubMed, g:Profiler and OpenAI. Your counts and sample names never leave this computer.
