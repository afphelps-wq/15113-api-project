Bulk RNA-seq Explorer

Upload a count matrix, compare two groups of samples, and see which genes differ — with supporting literature from PubMed.

1 Upload your data

Counts must be raw read counts, not normalized values (TPM, FPKM or CPM). Estimated counts with decimals are rounded automatically.

No data to hand? The repository includes demo_data/demo_counts.csv and demo_data/demo_metadata.csv — 60 pancreatic tumour samples from GEO series GSE205154.

2 Choose the comparison

Positive fold changes mean higher expression in the first group.

5 Literature context

Searches PubMed for each of the strongest genes, then asks a language model to interpret the results using only those abstracts.

Only gene symbols, fold changes and p-values are sent to PubMed, g:Profiler and OpenAI. Your counts and sample names never leave this computer.
